## Source and canon boundaries

- Source research used official Blizzard comic introductions first, then book-cited Warcraft Wiki plot/character passages where official comic renderers or PDFs were unavailable. Several direct Wiki requests were blocked; their indexed article text was consulted instead. The Sunwell Trilogy's additional plot details use the consulted English Wikipedia synopsis. This was not a panel-by-panel review of every original book.
- Facts, prompts, and correct choices are counted together against a 200-word ceiling per source across the whole bundle. Localization aliases of the same Blizzard article share one allowance. Structural audits cannot establish a lore fact's truth.
- Blackhand and Blood and Thunder are explicitly framed as alternate-Draenor comics. Chronicle 2 revised main-universe Blackhand's fire-transformed appearance; Blood and Thunder's ending also differs from main-universe Garad's fate. No cross-continuity claims are made.
- Son of the Wolf's elderly-Anduin ending is a possible future, not a guaranteed event, and is not quizzed. Warcraft Saga Issue 1's pre-Chronicle creation/corruption account is omitted. Its general Titan facts are already represented elsewhere in the bundle.
- No question depends on Dragon Hunt's superseded account of the Sunwell's destruction, Thassarian's disputed first enlistment timing, or the old Fenran name in Nightmares. Trag's nomadic Highmountain tribe is not equated with Huln's Broken Isles tribe.
- Movie-continuity comics, Hearthstone, RPG material, canceled volumes, and speculative future expansions are excluded. Legacy of Rage is included as a released Midnight comic, with an official Blizzard introduction and the cited character plot passage.
