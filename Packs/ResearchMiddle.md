## Continuity and wording decisions

- Official retrospective articles are evidence for unchanged story facts, not infallible references. A Cataclysm overview swaps dungeon-location blurbs; those blurbs were not used. Independent review also corrected Deathwing's claw-mark location from Stormwind Keep to the [gate towers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Valley_of_Heroes), and Wyrmrest Temple's makers from generic titans to [titan keepers](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Wyrmrest_Temple).
- Pandaria's previews and Classic retrospectives contribute story and Adventure Guide biographies only. Their old level requirements, release schedules, fight instructions and reward tables do not become questions.
- Draenor questions qualify incarnation-dependent events as alternate Draenor. A Durotan/Frostwolf leadership question was removed because it added almost no information beyond the existing RTS question, even though the timelines differ.
- Legion's Suramar details come from the campaign account rather than applying a character's later biography to every period. Comic-specific Highmountain events and relationships already covered in earlier-era files were kept out of this tranche.
- Dazar'alor has faction-specific presentations. Grong's question specifies his allegiance before undeath, and the Champion of the Light question specifies the Zandalari defender. The Golden Serpent's historical Hakkar-summoning context is Zandalar, not a claim that the ritual happened inside Kings' Rest. Brother Joseph and Sister Katherine are called a pair, not assumed to be blood siblings from their religious titles.
- Ny'alotha's Wrathion vision is not used as proof that the real Wrathion became the Black Emperor. The remaining Ny'alotha questions concern unambiguous inhabitants and biographies.
- Shadowlands uses explicit campaign revelations: the Runecarver's identity, Argus's effect on the Arbiter, Pelagos's choice and Sylvanas's penance. It does not infer a broader cosmological theory from those events.
- The two questions citing [Anduin's encounter page](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Anduin_Wrynn_%28tactics%29) use its embedded cinematic transcript, not the fight mechanics. Wording says Varian and Saurfang appear beside Anduin, without claiming these are their literal resident afterlife souls.
- The [Sisters' Tear](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Sisters%27_Tear) supplies the seed's origin and the rescued night elf souls' choice. Ysera's original Ardenweald revival is separate from Dragonflight's later exchange with Malfurion; planting, naming and the later World Tree finale belong to the Dragonflight file.

## Access and verification limits

Research used official Blizzard article text and Warcraft Wiki's cited quest, cinematic and Adventure Guide passages. Several direct Blizzard and Wiki requests returned access errors; in those cases their retrieved search-index text was consulted. Facts not visible in the retrieved passages were not treated as verified merely because a plausible URL existed. No claim is made that every linked page was accessible in full or that every quest was replayed in the live game.

Questions use original short wording rather than copied lore paragraphs. The catalogue audit caps derived prompt, correct-answer and explanation wording at 200 words per source, with Blizzard locale/domain aliases sharing a budget. It also checks IDs, byte bounds, choices and metadata. Those checks cannot establish canon, resolve a disputed interpretation or guarantee factual accuracy; subsequent corrections should preserve an ID only when its underlying question remains the same.

The authored files passed StyLua and the draft catalogue audit at handoff. Independent editorial review is a separate pass, and the root catalogue documentation records the integrated result.
